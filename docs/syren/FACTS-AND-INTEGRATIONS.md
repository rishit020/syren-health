# Syren content boundaries and remaining integration

The founder's adaptation brief is the authority for the product's stage and intended audience. Earlier local CAD files document prototype exploration, not a current integrated product specification or field validation.

## Public claims used

- Syren Health is developing room-based, contactless overnight monitoring for senior-care settings.
- The intended workflow is to sense activity, develop recognition of concerning patterns and support human caregiver assessment.
- Avoiding a resident wearable is a design goal.
- Privacy, resident dignity and caregiver judgment are design principles, not certification or performance claims.
- Hardware renders are concepts/prototypes, not evidence of manufacturing or clinical readiness.

## Claims withheld pending founder evidence

Current integrated sensor configuration; whether optical imagery is captured, stored or transmitted; detection sensitivity/accuracy/latency; clinical performance; installed pilots/customers; certifications, regulatory clearance, HIPAA compliance; pricing and savings. None are asserted on the page. Prior CAD reference envelopes do not establish these facts.

## Contact delivery

The current Vite/React repository has no configured email provider, contact API, server route or verified destination email address. The public form must explain that messages are not sent and offer an honest local action. Completing real delivery requires a founder-approved destination and a server-side endpoint/provider, configured outside the browser; never expose a provider secret in client code. Do not accept patient or sensitive medical information.

## Legal and historical template content

The inherited Luvia legal pages are template copy, not Syren policies. They must not be presented as Syren policies. Original reference assets, snapshots and QA evidence remain on disk as design provenance; old template pages are removed from the active public routing.

## Supplied product imagery

All five silver enclosure renders were supplied and visually inspected. Original files and SHA-256 identities are archived under `reference/syren-supplied/`. The front three-quarter and wider front/side views are used in the Device section; the internal electronics image remains off the public homepage. Images are resized proportionally and compressed to responsive WebP files without changing the hardware or its colors. They remain labeled concept/prototype renders. Their appearance is not evidence of an integrated sensor specification.
