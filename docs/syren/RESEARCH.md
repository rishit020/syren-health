# Adaptation references

Reviewed the live pages and browser screenshots on 2026-10-23. These informed page structure only; no competitor copy, visuals, specifications or claims were reused.

- [Luvia](https://luvia.framer.website/): primary visual authority. Existing captured DOM/CSS and comparison screenshots establish typography, cream/rust palette, softened surfaces, rounded buttons, editorial spacing and restrained entry motion.
- [Inspiren AUGi](https://www.inspiren.com/hardware/augi): physical hardware dominates the first viewport; the explanation connects the room device to the care team's next action.
- [Vayyar Care](https://vayyar.com/care/): product close-ups and workflow sections make ambient sensing tangible. Its sensor/privacy/performance claims are not evidence for Syren.
- [Nobi](https://www.nobi.life/): the device is presented as an object belonging in a resident's room; the primary action stays clear and addresses care operators.

## Decisions for Syren

Keep Luvia's visual system. Replace wellness proof, metrics, testimonials and stock portraits with the real Syren device and a short intended-workflow explanation. Keep development status prominent and describe privacy as a design priority. Use contact as an invitation for professional feedback, not an offer of an established commercial deployment.
